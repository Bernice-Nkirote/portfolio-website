About

01 / The story

“To bring about change you must not be afraid to take the first step.”

Rosa Parks

My professional journey began in procurement and supply chain management. Working in banking and supporting a family business gave me firsthand exposure to supplier coordination, purchasing records and the operational challenges that better systems can solve. My curiosity about digital procurement eventually led me to learn software development.

Today I'm developing Tendaflow, a procurement platform informed by those experiences, while continuing to build skills in data analytics. I also write SupplyByte, a LinkedIn series exploring how real companies use technology in procurement and supply chains. Outside work, I enjoy reading, creative projects and discovering new ideas.
